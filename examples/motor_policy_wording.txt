MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY WORDING

This document sets out the terms, conditions, and exclusions of your motor
insurance policy. Please read it alongside your policy schedule, which shows
the cover level, excesses, and named drivers that apply to you.

Section 1 — Windscreen and glass damage

1.1 What is covered

We will pay for repair or replacement of your vehicle's windscreen, windows,
or sunroof glass if it is cracked, chipped, or shattered as a result of an
accident, vandalism, or a road hazard such as a stone chip. Where the damage
can be repaired rather than replaced, we will always prefer repair, as this
keeps your no claims discount intact under most policy levels. A single
repair or replacement claim under this section does not count as a fault
claim for renewal pricing purposes, provided no other vehicle or property was
involved. You may use any approved glass repair network partner or your own
choice of repairer, subject to our standard labour rate cap.

1.2 What is not covered

We will not pay for glass damage that existed before your policy started, or
for scratches and surface marks that do not penetrate the glass. Damage
caused by deliberate acts by you or a named driver is excluded. If your
vehicle has aftermarket tinting or a head-up display film applied to the
windscreen, we will only reimburse the cost of standard replacement glass,
not the cost of re-applying the film or tint, unless you have added
tint cover separately to your policy.

Section 2 — Driving other cars (DOC)

2.1 What is covered

If your certificate of motor insurance shows "Driving Other Cars" cover, you
(the policyholder only, not named drivers) may drive a vehicle that you do
not own and that is not registered to you or leased to you, with the owner's
permission. This extension provides third-party only cover — it does not
cover damage to the car you are driving, fire, or theft. DOC cover only
applies if you also hold a comprehensive policy in your own name for a
vehicle you own, and it does not apply to vehicles provided to you by an
employer or under a lease or hire agreement.

2.2 What is not covered

DOC cover does not apply to drivers under 25, does not apply to any vehicle
belonging to a member of your household, and does not extend to named
drivers on your policy — only the policyholder is covered. It also does not
apply once your own comprehensive policy has lapsed or been cancelled. This
extension does not appear in every cover level; check your policy schedule
to confirm whether Driving Other Cars applies to your policy.

Section 3 — Business use

3.1 What is covered

Standard "social, domestic and pleasure" cover includes commuting to a
single fixed place of work. If your policy schedule shows "business use"
has been added, you are also covered for driving to multiple work sites,
carrying tools or samples related to your job, and travelling between
appointments as part of your normal duties, provided this is declared and
shown on your schedule.

3.2 What is not covered

Business use cover does not include hiring out your vehicle, carrying
passengers for payment (such as taxi, minicab, or ride-share work), courier
or delivery work, or any use classed as "hire and reward." Driving for these
purposes without the correct add-on invalidates your cover for that journey.
If your job role or driving pattern changes after your policy starts, you
must tell us — undeclared business use discovered after a claim may result
in the claim being reduced or refused under the terms of your policy.

Section 4 — Courtesy car

4.1 What is covered

If your vehicle is being repaired at an approved repairer following an
accident covered under your policy, we will provide a courtesy car for the
duration of the repair, subject to availability. The courtesy car is
provided on a like-for-basic basis — it will not necessarily match your own
vehicle's size, specification, or transmission type.

4.2 What is not covered

Courtesy car cover does not apply if your vehicle is being repaired for
mechanical breakdown, routine servicing, or MOT failure, none of which are
covered under this policy. It also does not apply if your vehicle is a total
loss, or if repairs are being carried out somewhere other than an approved
repairer. Third-party only and third-party fire and theft policies do not
include courtesy car cover.

Section 5 — Excesses

5.1 What is covered

Your policy schedule shows a compulsory excess and, if applicable, a
voluntary excess you chose when you took out the policy. These amounts are
added together and apply per claim, except for windscreen and glass claims
handled under Section 1, which carry their own separate, lower excess shown
on your schedule.

5.2 What is not covered

We will not waive or reduce your excess except where a claim is settled as
non-fault and the at-fault party's insurer has admitted full liability and
reimbursed us. Even where you are not at fault, you may need to pay your
excess upfront and be refunded once liability is confirmed by the other
insurer.

Section 6 — General exclusions

6.1 What is not covered

This policy does not cover: driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
above the legal limit; use of the vehicle for a purpose not declared on your
schedule; loss or damage happening outside the territorial limits shown on
your certificate of motor insurance; wear and tear, mechanical or electrical
breakdown; loss of value following repair; or any driver not named on your
policy and not covered by law to drive (for example, a driver without a
valid licence for the vehicle class).

Section 7 — No claims discount (NCD)

7.1 What is covered

Your no claims discount is earned for each consecutive year you do not make
a fault claim. NCD is shown on your policy schedule and confirmed separately
on an NCD confirmation letter from your current or previous insurer. NCD can
usually be protected for an additional premium, shown on your schedule as
"NCD protection," which allows a limited number of fault claims within a
five-year period without losing your discount, though your premium may still
rise.

7.2 What is not covered

Without NCD protection, one fault claim will reduce your no claims discount
according to our standard NCD scale, even where the claim amount is small.
NCD earned with a previous insurer is only honoured if evidenced by a valid
NCD confirmation letter dated within the last two years; we do not accept
verbal confirmation or self-declared NCD years without supporting evidence.

Section 8 — Making a claim

8.1 What is covered

You must report any accident, theft, fire, or malicious damage to us as
soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within 48 hours of it
happening. We will guide you through repair, replacement, or a total loss
settlement depending on the damage, and will handle correspondence with any
third party's insurer on your behalf where a third party was involved.

8.2 What is not covered

Claims reported more than 30 days after the event may be refused if the
delay has made it harder for us to investigate or has increased the cost of
the claim. We will not cover any loss arising from a deliberately false or
exaggerated claim, and doing so may result in the policy being cancelled
without refund.
